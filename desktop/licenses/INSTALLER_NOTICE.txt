NiSH MarkSteady Piano installs for your Windows user only. No administrator rights are needed.

NiSH MarkSteady Piano is free software, licensed under the GNU General Public License v3 or later.
Source code: https://github.com/nish-vibes/marksteady-wapiro
The full licence text is installed in resources\licenses\LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt.

Included third-party software:

Audiveris (optical music recognition), licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.
It runs as a separate program that NiSH MarkSteady Piano starts to read scanned sheet music.
Source code: https://github.com/Audiveris/audiveris
The full licence text is installed in resources\licenses\AUDIVERIS-LICENSE.txt.

Audiveris includes its own Java runtime.

pdf.js (Mozilla, Apache License 2.0) is built into the app to read PDF files.

Low-latency sound engine (nish-audio.exe): plays through ASIO drivers and reads MIDI keyboards directly.
It uses RtAudio by Gary P. Scavone (MIT-style licence, resources\licenses\RTAUDIO-LICENSE.txt) and the
ASIO host interface from the Steinberg ASIO SDK, used under the GNU General Public License v3.
ASIO is a trademark and software of Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH.
